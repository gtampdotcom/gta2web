Help and FAQ

What is this?

Grand Theft Auto 2 (DMA Design, 1999), running from the community's reconstruction of its original code, compiled for the web. It plays like the original, with modern extras: widescreen, a smoother camera, smooth motion on high-refresh screens, a map with waypoints, and co-op.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The first visit downloads the game once (about 355 MB) and keeps it in your browser, so later visits start straight away. Use a desktop browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari or Brave, on Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS.

How does co-op work?

Press "Host or join", host a room, and send your friends the link the lobby shows. When everyone is in, pick a district and start. Every player's browser runs the whole game; only key presses travel between you. Hold ~ (the key left of 1) for the scoreboard; your friends show on the map and radar in their own colours.

Where are my saves?

In this browser: they stay as long as you keep this site's data.

It runs slowly or looks wrong

Turn on the FPS counter in Options. Close other heavy tabs, and try another browser if it stays low. "Motion: Original 30 fps" and "Screen shape: 4:3 original" are the lightest settings.

Do you track me?

No cookies and no personal data. This site counts visits, games started, how long games run (in steps such as "over 30 minutes"), first-time downloads and which maps are hosted (with the host's country, not their address), anonymously, so we know how many people play. The counts are kept on this site (see them) and, where set up, with GoatCounter.
